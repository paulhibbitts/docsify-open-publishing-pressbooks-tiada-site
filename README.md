# Teaching in a Digital Age – Moved to Docsify-This

> The Pressbooks book [Teaching in a Digital Age: Third Edition](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/tonybates/), brought into Grav Open Publishing Space by the [Pressbooks Converter](https://pressbooks-converter.hibbittsdesign.org), then moved to [Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/#/) as plain Markdown pages by the [Open Publishing Course Space Bridge](https://open-publishing-course-space-bridge.hibbittsdesign.org).

View it as a website: https://docsify-this.net/?basePath=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-open-publishing-pressbooks-tiada-site/main&homepage=home.md&sidebar=true&loadSidebar=_sidebar.md&loadNavbar=_navbar.md&pagination=true&searchbox=true&collapsibleSidebarGroups=true&collapseSidebarGroups=true&dark-mode=auto&name=Teaching%20in%20a%20Digital%20Age&edit-link=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-open-publishing-pressbooks-tiada-site/main/home.md&edit-link-text=This%20page%20as%20Markdown&edit-link-emoji=:page_facing_up:

These pages are the bridge's output as it is, shown like the book in Open Publishing Space: the home page is the book's home page, starting with its cover, title, author and licence, then a card for each part. The sidebar starts with the book's name (a link back to its home page) and lists its chapters and pages, and Previous/Next buttons follow the pages in order.

The original book is by A.W. (Tony) Bates, licensed under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). The Pressbooks Converter's download went straight into the bridge, with no Grav site in between:

- `home.md` – the book's home page, with its cover image beside it
- `section-1/` – Front Matter
- `section-2/` … `section-15/` – Chapters 1 to 14, each with its overview (`README.md`) and pages
- `section-16/` – The book in a nutshell
- `section-17/` … `section-20/` – Appendices 1 to 4
- `section-21/` – Back Matter: bibliography, index and the book's Copyright page

Images stay on Pressbooks, as they do when the book is brought into Grav.

The pages are clean Markdown, without the bridge's hidden notes. The bridge can still turn them into an Open Publishing Space book, like any Docsify site, but not exactly.
